CAREER NAVIGATOR — INDIA-FOCUSED CAREER DATABASE NOTES

Primary occupation source:
Directorate General of Employment (DGE), Government of India — National Classification of Occupations (NCO) 2015.
Official searchable occupation catalogue: https://www.dge.gov.in/nat
Official NCO page and PDF volumes: https://www.dge.gov.in/nco-2015

Supplementary reference:
National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) — National Occupational Standards and Model Curriculum:
https://www.nsdcindia.org/national-occupational-standards-and-model-curriculum

How it works:
- The app reads the paginated official DGE occupation listing.
- It imports occupation titles, NCO codes, and classification groups into SQLite.
- Duplicate career titles are ignored.
- Starter skill mappings are assigned from occupation-family categories and should be reviewed before treating them as authoritative skill requirements.
- Recommendation logic iterates through all active careers stored in the database, not a fixed 25-career list.
- A successful source check is cached for 24 hours; failed requests are retried approximately every 15 minutes while the app process is running.

Accuracy statement for presentation:
NCO 2015 is a broad Indian occupation classification described by the Government of India as covering approximately 3,600 civilian occupations across 52 sectors. It is not a live vacancy feed and does not guarantee immediate discovery of every newly coined career. NSDC QP/NOS catalogues provide additional industry-defined job-role and skill references; the current importer checks the official NSDC catalogue page but does not claim to parse every linked qualification pack.

No ESCO or foreign job-board feed is used as the primary career database. No source can guarantee a complete, real-time list of every career in India.

Hosting limitation:
The scheduler runs inside the Flask application process. Free hosting services that sleep or restart cannot guarantee an exact 24-hour check. Use an always-on service or external scheduler for guaranteed execution.
